## Data-quality policy applied

- Text-explicit quantitative results are entered as measured A-level records when formulation/process linkage is explicit.
- Image-only tables are **not OCR-guessed** in this batch. Their sources remain B-level until a verified second pass.
- Claimed/source-level performance ranges are not assigned to individual examples.
- Process ranges are preserved in notes; any midpoint stored for a process convenience field is explicitly marked as such and must not be treated as a measured value.
- Missing MDI mass in CN111217992A is left missing; NCO/OH=2.2/1.0 is preserved instead.
